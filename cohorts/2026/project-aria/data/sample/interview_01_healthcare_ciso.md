speaker: Dr. Elena Ruiz (CISO, regional hospital network)
Interviewer: How is your organization approaching AI adoption right now?
Elena: Carefully. We're a regulated environment, so anything that touches patient
data has to be auditable end to end. My biggest fear isn't the model being wrong
once — it's not being able to explain, six months later, why it did what it did.
Determinism and traceability beat raw capability for us every time.
Interviewer: Where do coding agents fit?
Elena: We pilot them in sandboxes, never against production PHI. The value is real
for boilerplate and test generation, but the governance wrapper around the agent
matters more than the agent itself. If I can't gate it, I can't ship it.
