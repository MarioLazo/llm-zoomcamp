## How to read this tracker

- **Complete** — item closed, evidence on file.
- **On track** — in progress, no expected delay against target date.
- **At risk** — in progress, meaningful chance of missing target date without intervention.
- **Overdue** — target date has passed without closure.

Any item shown as **Overdue** or **At risk** must be explicitly called out in
the next Board compliance briefing — silently omitting a slipping item from
a board report is itself a governance gap, which is the exact failure mode
finding 8 was issued for.
