## Repayment Source Analysis

Primary repayment source is annual grain and soybean harvest revenue,
supplemented by a federal crop insurance policy (RMA policy #IL-44219,
current). Five-year historical yield data reviewed against county averages
shows the operation tracking 6-8% above county mean yield per acre for both
crops. Secondary repayment source is off-farm income from a co-signer's
salaried position (verified via two most recent pay stubs and a signed
employment letter, on file). Debt service coverage ratio calculated at 1.34x
using a 3-year average net farm income, above the bank's 1.20x policy
minimum for ag operating lines.

## Cash Flow Analysis

Trailing 3-year farm financial statements (2023-2025) reviewed. Working
capital position is adequate (current ratio 1.6x). Seasonal cash flow
projection for the 2026 crop year prepared using current futures pricing
with a 10% downside stress case; projection shows the line fully repaid by
harvest settlement in all three pricing scenarios modeled. No material
unexplained cash flow gaps identified.

## Collateral Valuation

Collateral consists of a first lien on 640 acres of tillable farmland
(appraised value $4,480,000, appraisal dated 2025-11-02, USPAP-compliant,
performed by an independent state-certified ag appraiser) plus a blanket UCC
lien on farm equipment and growing crops (estimated value $612,000 per
current insurance schedule). Combined loan-to-value is 36%, well within
policy. Title search and lien search both current and clean as of the
renewal date.
