# Loan Underwriting File — Riverside Construction Group Inc.

**Loan type:** C&I term loan, new equipment (crane + concrete plant)
**Loan amount:** $2,100,000
**Origination date:** 2026-05-22
**Underwriter:** T. Osei, Commercial Lender

## Repayment Source Analysis

Primary repayment source is contract revenue from Riverside's existing
municipal and commercial construction backlog, currently $9.4M across 6
active contracts. Backlog schedule and two largest contracts (municipal
bridge repair, commercial warehouse build-out) reviewed and reconciled to
the company's percentage-of-completion accounting. Historical gross margin
on completed contracts over the trailing 24 months averages 14.2%,
consistent with the borrower's projections. Debt service coverage projected
at 1.28x on the new debt layered onto existing obligations, above the
bank's 1.20x C&I policy minimum.

## Cash Flow Analysis

Borrower-prepared 2026 cash flow projection reviewed at a high level during
the credit committee meeting; a full trailing 3-year cash flow
reconciliation to tax returns and interim financials was requested from the
borrower but had not been received as of loan closing. Loan officer notes
in the file indicate the borrower's accountant would submit the
reconciliation "before the first quarterly compliance certification," which
has not yet occurred as of this file's last update. No independent cash
flow stress test has been performed for this credit to date.

## Collateral Valuation

Collateral is the crane and concrete plant being financed, plus a
second-lien blanket UCC filing on Riverside's other equipment. Purchase
invoices for the new equipment are on file ($2,340,000 combined, supporting
an 89.7% advance rate). Independent third-party equipment appraisal has not
been ordered; the loan-to-value calculation above relies on vendor invoice
price rather than an appraised fair market value, which is a deviation from
standard policy for equipment loans over $1,000,000.

## Underwriter's recommendation

Approve with conditions — see credit committee minutes 2026-05-20. Cash
flow reconciliation and independent equipment appraisal remain outstanding
as of file close-out.
