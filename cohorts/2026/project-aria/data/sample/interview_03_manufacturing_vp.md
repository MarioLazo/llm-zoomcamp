speaker: Priya Nandakumar (VP Engineering, industrial manufacturer)
Interviewer: What changed your mind about generative AI?
Priya: Watching a junior engineer ship a week of work in a day with a coding
assistant. But I also watched the same tool confidently produce code that violated
our safety interlocks. So we invested in evaluation. If you can't measure the
failure rate, you're gambling, and we don't gamble on the factory floor.
Interviewer: How do you build trust with the team?
Priya: Transparency. Show people the monitoring, the feedback loop, the numbers.
People trust a system they can see being measured. Fear comes from the black box.
