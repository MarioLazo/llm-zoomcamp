speaker: Tomás Okafor (Privacy counsel, tech-sector advisor)
Interviewer: What do companies get wrong about AI and privacy?
Tomás: They treat privacy as a checkbox at the end instead of a design constraint
at the start. If you architect for data minimization and consent up front, you move
faster later. Retrofitting privacy onto a shipped system is the most expensive path
there is.
Interviewer: Advice for teams building with interview or customer data?
Tomás: Never train or index on data you don't have clear rights to. Anonymize,
get consent, and keep the sensitive corpus private. The demo can run on synthetic
data — the real value doesn't require exposing real people.
