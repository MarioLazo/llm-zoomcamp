speaker: Marcus Bell (Head of Data, mid-size bank)
Interviewer: What's the hardest part of AI in a bank?
Marcus: Trust and lineage. Regulators want to know where every number came from.
We use retrieval-augmented systems so answers are grounded in our own documents,
not the model's imagination. A hallucinated figure in a financial report is not a
bug, it's an incident.
Interviewer: Agents or deterministic pipelines?
Marcus: For reporting, deterministic, always. Agents are for exploration and
internal productivity. The moment compliance is involved, I want a workflow I can
replay and audit, not a model deciding its own path.
